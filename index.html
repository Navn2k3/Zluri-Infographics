Competitive Research & Analysis

Informing the Zluri Employee App Catalog

The purpose of this research is to analyze existing app catalog solutions to understand best practices, identify key user experience patterns, and extract actionable insights. The findings will directly inform the design and development of Zluri's new Employee App Catalog feature.

By examining leaders in both enterprise and consumer markets, we can identify proven strategies for application discovery, user engagement, and information architecture. This ensures Zluri's solution is intuitive, effective, and drives employee adoption, ultimately reducing Shadow IT and streamlining app provisioning.
